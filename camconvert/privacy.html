Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026. App: CamConvert (com.dilaradev.camconvert).

Controller

The app is published by Mendi Tekstilwaren / Dilara Demir Caglar, Fauststrasse 44a, 81827 München, Germany. Contact: support@qrodesk.com.

Camera

CamConvert uses the camera to read prices. The text recognition runs on your device. No photo or video is saved, and no camera image leaves your phone. You can use the typed converter without granting camera access.

Stored only on your device

Your chosen currencies, the saved exchange rates and the count of today's free scans stay on your device. There is no account and nothing is uploaded.

Exchange rates

To update rates the app downloads a public rate table from Frankfurter (api.frankfurter.dev) and, if that is unavailable, from the currency-api project served by jsDelivr. These requests contain no personal data or identifiers from the app; as with any internet request, the provider sees your IP address.

Purchases

CamConvert Pro (subscription or one-time purchase) is processed by Apple. We never see your payment details. To verify purchases the app uses RevenueCat (RevenueCat, Inc.), which receives an anonymous app user ID, purchase receipts and technical device information. No name or email address is sent. See RevenueCat's privacy policy.

Ads and tracking

CamConvert contains no ads, no tracking and no analytics SDK.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your country), and we do not knowingly collect data from children.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and objection, and the right to complain to a supervisory authority such as the Bayerisches Landesamt für Datenschutzaufsicht (BayLDA). Since the app keeps your data on your device, deleting the app removes it. For purchase records held by RevenueCat, write to us.

Contact
