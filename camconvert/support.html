CamConvert Support

CamConvert reads a price with your camera and shows it in your own currency.

Questions or a price it reads wrong? Write to support@qrodesk.com. A photo of the price tag helps.

The camera does not lock on a price

Hold the price inside the brackets in the middle of the view, about a hand's length away, and keep the phone still for a moment. In dim light turn on the flashlight (top right). Handwritten prices are often not readable; type the amount instead.

The converted amount looks wrong

Check the two currencies in the bottom panel: left is the currency on the tag, right is yours. Rates are daily reference rates, so your bank or card may charge a slightly different rate plus fees.

Does it work without internet?

Yes. Rates are saved on your phone and the date they are from is shown under the result. Open the app once with a connection before your trip to have the latest rates.

Camera access is off

Open the iPhone Settings app, choose CamConvert and turn on Camera. Typing an amount works without it.

Purchases

Restore a purchase in the app under Settings, Restore purchases. Subscriptions are managed and cancelled in the iPhone Settings app under your Apple Account, Subscriptions. Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.
