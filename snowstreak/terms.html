Terms of Use

Last updated: October 16, 2026

Service scope

SnowStreak is a ski-season app with two halves: a six-week bodyweight training program of short guided sessions you do before the season, and a log for the ski days you take during it. All content is bundled in the app and all your data stays on your device — there is no account, no sign-in and no cloud sync.

Not medical advice

SnowStreak is a fitness training and tracking tool, not medical advice. Consult a physician before starting a new exercise program — especially if you have had a knee, hip or back injury. Stop immediately and seek medical attention if you experience pain. Skiing and snowboarding carry inherent risk of serious injury; completing this program does not make you safe on the mountain.

No account

  • No registration is required, and we store nothing about you on a server.
  • Your progress and ski day log live only on the device you use. Uninstalling the app, resetting it, or losing the device erases that data permanently — we cannot recover it for you.
  • You are responsible for your device's own backup (iCloud / Google) if you want to keep that data.

User responsibilities

  • Train within your physical ability, warm up as instructed, and follow the session cues responsibly.
  • Pick the level (beginner or advanced) that matches your actual fitness, not your ambition.
  • Do not use SnowStreak for any unlawful purpose, and do not attempt to reverse engineer, modify or redistribute the app or its bundled exercise media.

Free features

SnowStreak is free to use without a subscription: week 1 of the program is fully available and you can log up to five ski days. Free feature availability may change over time; we will not remove access you have already paid for.

SnowStreak Premium

Premium unlocks weeks 2–6 of the program, an unlimited ski day log, pass cost per day with the shareable season recap card, and CSV export. It comes in two forms, billed through the App Store on iOS or Google Play on Android — whichever platform you bought it on:

  • Season Pass — a yearly auto-renewing subscription, which starts with a 7-day free trial. You are charged the price shown on the purchase screen when the trial ends, then once a year.
  • Lifetime — a one-time purchase that never renews and is never charged again.

Prices are shown in your local currency before you confirm. The Season Pass renews automatically at the then-current price unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period — cancelling during the trial avoids any charge. Manage or cancel it from your Apple ID or Google Play account settings; deleting the app does not cancel a subscription. Restore purchases is available in Settings and on the purchase screen, and works with the same Apple ID or Google account used for the original purchase.

Refunds are handled by Apple or Google under their own policies — we cannot issue them directly.
