Pre-season training & season log

Be ready on day one.

Every season starts the same way: three runs and your legs are done. SnowStreak gives you six weeks of short bodyweight sessions before the first lift — then logs every ski day you take after it.

Download on the App Store Support

Android version coming to Google Play.

Six weeks, three sessions

17–22 minutes each, no gym and no equipment. Three rotating sessions build leg strength, knee stability and the endurance for a full afternoon on the piste.

Beginner or advanced

Every session has two versions. A guided player runs the work and rest timers with one cue per exercise and a demo clip.

A streak that counts weeks

Weeks, not days — so a rest day never breaks it. The home screen counts down to your first ski day.

Then log the season

Resort, conditions, rating and a note per day. Add your season pass price and SnowStreak shows what each ski day actually cost you, plus a shareable recap card.

No account, nothing uploaded

No sign-up, no sync, no analytics, no ads. Your training progress and ski days stay on your device.

Twelve languages

English, German, French, Italian, Spanish, Dutch, Czech, Polish, Norwegian, Swedish, Finnish and Turkish.

Free and Premium

Week 1 of the program and five logged ski days are free. Premium unlocks weeks 2–6, an unlimited ski day log, pass cost per day with the recap card, and CSV export — as Season Pass (yearly, with a 7-day free trial) or a one-time Lifetime purchase.
