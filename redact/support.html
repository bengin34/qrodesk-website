Blackproof Support

Blackproof removes personal data from PDF files on your Mac and checks the result before you share it.

Questions, or a document that was not cleaned well? Write to support@qrodesk.com. Please do not send personal documents; describe the problem or send a made-up example instead.

Something was not found

Draw a box over it, or add the word under Settings, Detection, Always remove. Scanned pages need "Read text in pictures and scans" switched on.

Too much is selected

Untick the finding in the list, or add the word under Settings, Detection, Never remove. A kind you never want selected can be switched off in the same tab.

Is the text really gone?

Yes. The text, image parts and hidden extras such as author, attachments and form data are deleted, not covered. Every exported file is opened again and checked; the proof shows each check. "What others see" shows the file as the recipient gets it.

Does it work offline?

Yes. The app has no network access at all. Your documents never leave your Mac.

Purchases

Finding, reviewing and "What others see" are free. One purchase unlocks saving the cleaned file. Restore it in the app under Settings, Purchase, Restore Purchase. Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.
