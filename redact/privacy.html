Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 November 2026. App: Blackproof for Mac (com.dilaradev.redact).

Controller

The app is published by Mendi Tekstilwaren / Dilara Demir Caglar, Fauststrasse 44a, 81827 München, Germany. Contact: support@qrodesk.com.

Your documents stay on your Mac

Blackproof finds, removes and checks personal data in PDF files only on your Mac. No document, and no data from a document, is uploaded, sent or stored anywhere else. The app has no network access: it is not granted the network permission, so macOS blocks every connection. Text in scanned pages is read with the text recognition built into macOS, also on your Mac.

Files

The app reads only the files you open and writes only the files you save. The original file is never changed. Temporary files are kept in the app's own sandbox container and deleted after use. Your settings (for example words to always remove) are stored on your Mac.

No data collected

We collect no personal data, no usage statistics and no identifiers. There is no account, no analytics, no ads and no tracking.

Purchases

The one-time purchase that unlocks export is processed by Apple through the App Store. The app checks it on your Mac with Apple's StoreKit. We never receive your name, Apple Account or payment details.

Crash reports from Apple

If you have chosen in macOS to share analytics with app developers, Apple may send us anonymous crash reports. They contain technical information, never your documents. You can change this in System Settings, Privacy & Security, Analytics & Improvements.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your country), and we do not knowingly collect data from children.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and objection, and the right to complain to a supervisory authority such as the Bayerisches Landesamt für Datenschutzaufsicht (BayLDA). Since the app keeps everything on your Mac, deleting the app removes its data.
