FAQ

Do I need an account?

No. WortJagd works without sign-up. Your progress, streaks and word list are stored on your device.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Every word ships with the app, so the daily puzzle, practice and arcade modes all work without an internet connection. Only purchases and restoring them need a connection.

Which level is WortJagd for?

Everyday words cover A1 to B2, and you can choose one level to train in Settings. Profession vocabulary is built for people working, or preparing to work, in Germany.

Can I learn another language than German?

No. German is always the language you learn. The ten languages are the ones you can play in: they translate the interface and every word's meaning.

What does WortJagd Pro unlock?

Unlimited practice runs, unlimited arcade runs, changing your profession any time, and review-first practice for the words you got wrong. The daily puzzle stays free without Pro.
