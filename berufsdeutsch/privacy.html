Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 17, 2026

This policy explains which data the WorkDeutsch app (BerufsDeutsch, iOS and Android) and this website process, why, who receives it, how long it is kept and how you can withdraw your consent. The app has no user accounts: we never ask for your name, email address or phone number in the app.

Controller

Mendi Tekstilwaren / Dilara Demir Caglar, Fauststrasse 44a, 81827 Muenchen, Germany. Email: support@qrodesk.com. See also our Impressum.

Data stored only on your device

Your profession, German level, learning goal, learning progress, flashcard review state, personal vocabulary (including files you import), streaks and settings are stored locally on your device. A random identifier (a UUID generated by the app, not linked to your name, email or device hardware) is also stored locally. Daily reminders are local notifications scheduled on your device; no push token is sent to us. Text-to-speech uses your device's built-in speech engine.

Usage analytics (Google Firebase Analytics) – only with your consent

If, and only if, you agree on the consent screen during onboarding or later switch on "Anonyme Nutzungsdaten" in Settings, the app sends usage events to Google Firebase Analytics (Google Ireland Limited / Google LLC). Analytics collection is disabled by default and stays off until you agree.

  • Events such as screens viewed, lessons started, completed or abandoned, flashcard reviews, quiz answers, XP and streak updates, paywall views and outcomes, content requests and ratings, and app error messages (message text only, no stack trace).
  • User properties: selected profession, German level, learning goal and whether you use the free or Pro version.
  • The random app identifier described above, set as the analytics user ID.
  • Data the Firebase SDK collects automatically, such as device model, operating system and app version, app instance ID, language and coarse location derived from your IP address. On iOS, the advertising identifier (IDFA) is only available to the SDK if you allow tracking in the iOS App Tracking Transparency prompt.

Purpose: understanding which content helps learners and which professions need more content. Legal basis: your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR). Data may be processed by Google in the USA; Google relies on the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework and Standard Contractual Clauses. Retention: user-level analytics data is kept according to the Google Analytics data-retention setting of this project, at most 14 months, and then deleted. Firebase privacy information.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version shows banner and optional rewarded ads served by Google AdMob (Google Ireland Limited / Google LLC). Pro users do not see banner ads. The app requests non-personalised ads only. To deliver, cap the frequency of, measure and protect ads against fraud, Google receives device and network data such as IP address, device model, operating system, app version and ad interactions.

  • Consent (Google UMP): In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, and wherever else required, a Google User Messaging Platform consent form is shown before ads are requested. You can review or change that choice at any time in Settings → Datenschutz → "Werbe-Datenschutz verwalten".
  • App Tracking Transparency (iOS): The app asks for permission to track. Only if you tap "Allow" can the advertising identifier (IDFA) be used, to measure ad performance and to show you the same ad less often. If you decline, the IDFA is not available to the app or to Google. You can change this at any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Android: AdMob may use the Android advertising ID. You can reset or delete it in your device's Google / Ads settings.

Legal basis: your consent where required (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR, § 25 TDDDG); otherwise our legitimate interest in financing the free version (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). Retention is governed by Google's policies. How Google uses data from apps that use its services.

Subscriptions (RevenueCat, Apple, Google)

Purchases are processed by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play); we never receive your payment details. To manage Pro subscriptions and restore purchases, the app uses RevenueCat (RevenueCat, Inc., USA), which receives an anonymous RevenueCat app user ID, purchase and subscription receipts/history, product identifiers, device type, operating system, app version, country/storefront and IP address. Legal basis: performance of the contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR). Data is kept as long as needed to provide the subscription and to meet legal retention obligations. RevenueCat privacy policy.

Content requests ("Mehr Inhalte anfragen")

When you actively tap to request more content for a profession, the app sends a small request to a webhook endpoint we operate to collect these requests. It contains only the random app identifier, the selected profession, the requested content type and the time of the request – no name, email or device identifier. We use it only to decide which content to create next and delete the requests once they are no longer needed for that purpose. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in improving the content (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). Nothing is sent unless you tap the request button.

Support and email contact

If you email us (for example via "Kontakt", "Fehler melden" or "Mein Beruf fehlt" in the app), we receive your email address and whatever you include, plus the app version and platform the app pre-fills in the email. We use this only to answer your request and delete it once the request is resolved, unless legal retention duties apply.

Website data

This website is a static website. It does not use advertising trackers or third-party analytics scripts. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which means your browser sends your IP address to Google when loading this page.

Withdrawing consent and deleting data

  • Analytics: switch off "Anonyme Nutzungsdaten" in Settings → Datenschutz. Collection stops immediately.
  • Ads consent: Settings → Datenschutz → "Werbe-Datenschutz verwalten" (shown where a consent form applies).
  • Tracking (iOS): iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Local data: deleting the app removes all data stored on your device, including the random app identifier.
  • Data held by us or our processors: email support@qrodesk.com (if possible, with the app identifier shown in your request) and we will delete it or arrange its deletion.

Your rights

You have the right to access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and objection, and to withdraw consent at any time with effect for the future. You can also lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority, for example the Bayerisches Landesamt für Datenschutzaufsicht (BayLDA).

Children

The app is intended for adults learning German for work and is not directed at children.

Changes

We update this policy when the app's data processing changes. The date above shows the latest version.
