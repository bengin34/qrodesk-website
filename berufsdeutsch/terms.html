WorkDeutsch Pro subscription

  • Pro is an optional auto-renewable subscription. The price and period are shown on the purchase screen before you confirm.
  • Payment is charged to your Apple Account (or Google Play account) when you confirm the purchase.
  • The subscription renews automatically unless it is cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. Your account is charged for the renewal within 24 hours before the current period ends.
  • You can manage or cancel the subscription at any time in your Apple Account settings (iPhone Settings > your name > Subscriptions) or in the Google Play Store. Cancelling stops the next renewal; Pro stays active until the end of the paid period.
  • If a free trial is offered, any unused part of it ends when you buy a subscription.
  • Previous purchases can be restored in the app under Settings > Subscription ("Käufe wiederherstellen"). Refunds are handled by Apple or Google under their policies.
