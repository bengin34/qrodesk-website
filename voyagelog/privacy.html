Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 17, 2026

VoyageLog is designed to keep your travel history private. The app works offline-first and does not require an account. We (the developer) do not run any server that receives your trips, entries, photos or locations.

What data is stored

Everything you enter is stored in a local database on your device: trips, cities, entries, notes, tags, ratings, weather notes, expenses, city lists, badges and your app settings. Photos and videos you add are stored in the app's private storage on your device.

Where your data lives

Your data stays on your device. We do not collect, copy or sell it, and we cannot see it. If you delete the app, its data is deleted with it unless you have made an export or backup.

Camera, photos, microphone and location

VoyageLog only asks for these permissions when you use the related feature:

  • Camera – to take photos or record videos for a trip entry.
  • Microphone – to record sound while you capture a video for an entry (iOS).
  • Photo library – to add existing photos or videos to your trips.
  • Location (while using the app) – to tag an entry with your current position. Coordinates are saved only on your device. To turn coordinates into a place name, the app uses your device's built-in geocoding service (Apple on iOS, Google on Android), which receives the coordinates for that lookup. VoyageLog never tracks your location in the background.

You can change any of these permissions at any time in your device settings.

Maps and map links

Maps inside the app are provided by your platform's map service (Apple Maps on iOS, Google Maps on Android), which loads map tiles over the internet. If you paste a shortened Google Maps link, the app contacts Google once to expand it. Opening a place in a maps app hands it to that app.

Purchases (RevenueCat)

VoyageLog Pro is sold through the App Store or Google Play, which handle payment. We use RevenueCat to validate purchases and restore them. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user ID, your purchase and subscription history, and basic device information (such as OS version, app version and country/storefront). It does not receive your travel data. We never see your payment details.

Exporting and backing up your data

You can export all of your data as JSON, as a ZIP archive that includes your photos and videos, or as a spreadsheet (XLS), and you can share individual trips, guides and recaps as PDF or image files. The iCloud backup option creates a backup file and opens the system share sheet so you can save it to iCloud Drive or any location you choose. Exports and backups go only where you send them; once saved or shared, the service you chose (for example iCloud Drive or a messaging app) handles them under its own privacy policy.

Notifications

If you enable them, memory flashbacks and monthly recaps are local notifications scheduled on your device. No push service is used.

No tracking

VoyageLog does not include advertising, analytics or tracking SDKs and does not track you across apps or websites.

Crash reports

We do not collect crash reports. If you share diagnostics with developers through Apple or Google, that is governed by their settings and policies.

Children

VoyageLog is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect personal data from anyone.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the “Last updated” date above.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Email us at support@qrodesk.com.
