Support

Potty Pip is a simple potty training log for parents: tap what happened, get a small celebration and a star for each success, and spot patterns over time.

Email support: support@qrodesk.com

We usually reply within 2 working days. Please include your device model, iOS version and app version.

Getting started

  1. Open the Today tab and tap what happened: Pee, Poo, Both, Accident, Tried or Dry.
  2. Long press a button to log an earlier time.
  3. Tap Undo right after logging if you tapped the wrong one.
  4. Open Stars and Patterns to see progress. Patterns, with the week at a glance, time of day and accident-free days, needs Potty Pip Pro.

Common questions

How do reminders work?

Turn reminders on in Settings and choose an interval between 15 and 120 minutes. You can set quiet hours, pause reminders, or snooze them. Reminders are local notifications; iOS asks for notification permission the first time you turn them on.

Reminders do not appear.

Check that notifications are allowed for Potty Pip in iOS Settings, that reminders are on and not paused or snoozed in the app, and that the time is not inside your quiet hours. Focus modes and Do Not Disturb can also silence them.

How do I share a day's log?

Sharing needs Potty Pip Pro. Use the share button on the day you want. The app creates a plain-text summary and opens the system share sheet, so you can send it to a partner, a caregiver or a daycare. Nothing is shared unless you tap share.

Where is my data stored?

Your log is stored only on your device. There is no account. The app keeps automatic backup files locally on the device. Details: Privacy Policy.

Can I move my log to a new phone?

Yes, on iPhone it comes along with your device backup. If you set up the new phone from an iCloud or computer backup, or with Quick Start, the log is restored with the app. With Potty Pip Pro you can also export your data from Settings; the app has no in-app import.

How do I delete my data?

Delete the app. This removes the log, settings and local backup files from your device. We have no copy of your log. For purchase records, see the Privacy Policy.

Is it really free?

Free to use: logging, celebrations, the star chart and reminders cost nothing. The Patterns tab, sharing a day's log and data export need Potty Pip Pro, a one-time in-app purchase. No ads and no account.

How do I restore Potty Pip Pro?

Open Settings and tap Restore purchases under Potty Pip Pro, signed in to the same store account you bought it with. Refunds are handled by the store.

Which languages does the app support?

English, German, Spanish, French, Italian, Japanese, Portuguese and Turkish.
