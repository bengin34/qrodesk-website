Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 November 2026. App: Potty Pip: Potty Training (com.dilaradev.pottylog).

Controller

The app is published by Mendi Tekstilwaren / Dilara Demir Caglar, Fauststrasse 44a, 81827 München, Germany. Contact: support@qrodesk.com.

The short version

Everything you log in Potty Pip stays on your device. There is no account, no server, no analytics, no crash reporting, no ads and no tracking. We do not receive your data.

What the app stores on your device

  • The potty events you log (pee, poo, both, accident, tried, dry), with date and time.
  • Stars and progress, and your settings (for example reminder interval, quiet hours, pause and snooze).
  • Automatic backup files that the app writes locally on the device, so your log survives an app restart. They are not uploaded anywhere by the app.

Potty Pip is used by parents. It does not ask for or store a name, a photo or any identifier of your child.

Reminders

Reminders are local notifications scheduled on your device. The app asks for notification permission only when you turn reminders on. You can change this at any time in iOS Settings. No notification content or schedule is sent to us or to a push service.

Sharing a day's log

If you tap share, the app creates a plain-text summary of that day and opens the system share sheet. Where it goes (Messages, Mail, Notes, a file and so on) is your choice and is handled by the app you pick. Nothing is shared unless you tap share.

Purchases, ads and tracking

Potty Pip is free. It has no in-app purchases, no ads, no tracking and no analytics or crash-reporting SDK. The app bundles the RevenueCat SDK, but it is not configured in this version: it makes no purchases and sends no data. We do not use any third party to collect data about you.

Children

The app is meant to be used by parents and caregivers, not by children. We do not knowingly collect any data from children, and the app stores no names, photos or identifiers of the child.

Deleting your data

All data is on your device, so deleting the app deletes the log, settings and local backup files. We hold no copy and have nothing to delete on our side.

Your rights

Because we do not receive or store your data, there is no personal data on our side to access or erase. Under the GDPR you still have the rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and objection, and you can lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority, for example the Bayerisches Landesamt für Datenschutzaufsicht (BayLDA).

Changes

If the app changes how it handles data (for example if a future version adds purchases or a sync feature), we will update this page and the date above before that version is released.
