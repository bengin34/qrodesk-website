Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 November 2026. App: Potty Pip: Potty Training (com.dilaradev.pottylog).

Controller

The app is published by Mendi Tekstilwaren / Dilara Demir Caglar, Fauststrasse 44a, 81827 München, Germany. Contact: support@qrodesk.com.

The short version

Everything you log in Potty Pip stays on your device, and we do not receive it. There is no account, no ads, no tracking, and no analytics or crash-reporting SDK. The optional Potty Pip Pro purchase is handled by Apple or Google Play and RevenueCat, which receive purchase and technical data as described below.

What the app stores on your device

  • The potty events you log (pee, poo, both, accident, tried, dry), with date and time.
  • Stars and progress, and your settings (for example reminder interval, quiet hours, pause and snooze).
  • Automatic backup files that the app writes locally on the device, so your log survives an app restart. They are not uploaded anywhere by the app. If you use iCloud Backup or back up your iPhone to a computer, the app's data is part of that device backup, which Apple's or your own settings control; we have no access to it.

Potty Pip is used by parents. It does not ask for or store a name, a photo or any identifier of your child.

Reminders

Reminders are local notifications scheduled on your device. The app asks for notification permission only when you turn reminders on. You can change this at any time in iOS Settings. No notification content or schedule is sent to us or to a push service.

Sharing a day's log

With Potty Pip Pro, if you tap share, the app creates a plain-text summary of that day and opens the system share sheet. Where it goes (Messages, Mail, Notes, a file and so on) is your choice and is handled by the app you pick. Nothing is shared unless you tap share.

Purchases

Potty Pip is free to use. Potty Pip Pro is an optional one-time in-app purchase that unlocks the Patterns tab, sharing a day's log and data export. Payment is handled by Apple's App Store (Google Play on Android). We never see your card or payment details.

To process and restore the purchase, the app uses RevenueCat (RevenueCat, Inc., USA). The app contacts RevenueCat when it runs, even if you buy nothing. RevenueCat receives:

  • purchase history, including the Apple receipt or Google purchase token,
  • an anonymous app user ID that RevenueCat generates (not your name or email),
  • basic device and app information, such as device type, operating system, app version, language or locale, currency and when the app was last seen,
  • your IP address, which any internet connection transmits.

Your potty log, stars and settings are not sent to RevenueCat. We can see purchase records and purchase statistics in RevenueCat. The legal basis is providing the purchase you ask for and our legitimate interest in running it (Art. 6(1)(b) and (f) GDPR). See RevenueCat's privacy policy and Apple's or Google's.

Ads and tracking

Potty Pip has no ads, no tracking and no analytics or crash-reporting SDK.

Children

The app is meant to be used by parents and caregivers, not by children. We do not knowingly collect any data from children, and the app stores no names, photos or identifiers of the child.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app deletes the log, settings and local backup files from your device. We hold no copy of them. Purchase records held by Apple, Google or RevenueCat are not removed by deleting the app. To ask for deletion of RevenueCat records, write to us.

Your rights

We do not receive your log. For purchase records held by RevenueCat, write to us. Under the GDPR you have the rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and objection, and you can lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority, for example the Bayerisches Landesamt für Datenschutzaufsicht (BayLDA).

Changes

If the app changes how it handles data (for example if a future version adds a sync feature), we will update this page and the date above before that version is released.

Contact

Privacy questions: support@qrodesk.com.
